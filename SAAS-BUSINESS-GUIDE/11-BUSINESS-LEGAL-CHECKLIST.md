# ⚖️ Business, Legal & Compliance Master Checklist
## Establishing QLLEX Technologies as a Trusted IT Brand in India

Software banane ke baad commercially legally compliant hona zaruri hai taaki kal ko payment gateway blocks, GST penalties, ya client disputes na hon.

Is guide mein **QLLEX Technologies** ke business launch ke liye legal roadmap explain kiya gaya hai.

---

## 1. Business Entity Structure: Kya Register Karein?

| Entity Type | Initial Setup Cost | Compliances | Liability Protection | Recommendation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sole Proprietorship** | ₹1,000 – ₹2,500 | Very low | Personal liability | 🟢 **Best to start in Month 1** |
| **LLP (Limited Liability Partnership)** | ₹5,000 – ₹8,000 | Low to Moderate | Limited liability | 🟡 Good if 2+ founders |
| **Private Limited (Pvt Ltd)** | ₹7,000 – ₹15,000 | High (Annual Audits) | Highest | 🔵 Best after crossing ₹10L ARR |

### Step-by-Step Entity Launch for Beginners:
1. **Month 1 (Sole Proprietorship):**
   - Apne local municipal corporation se **Udyam / MSME Registration** (Free online on udyamregistration.gov.in) aur **Shop & Establishment Act (Gumasta Dhara)** certificate lein.
   - In do documents se aapke company name ("QLLEX Technologies") ke naam par **Current Bank Account** open ho jata hai.
2. **Phase 2 (Upgrade to Pvt Ltd):**
   - Jab aapke paas 25+ regular paying hotels ho jayein, tab business ko *QLLEX Technologies Private Limited* mein convert karein.

## 2. GST (Goods & Services Tax) Rules for SaaS in India

- **Mandatory Threshold:** Services business ke liye all-India threshold **₹20 Lakhs annual turnover** hai (Gujarat inclusive).
- **Initial Stage (< ₹20 Lakhs):** Agar aapka revenue ₹20 Lakhs se kam hai, toh legal requirement nahi hai.
- **Voluntary GST Registration (Recommended):**
  - Agar aap GST registration le lete hain (18% SAC code 998313 - Information Technology Software Services):
  - **Fayda 1:** B2B restaurants aapko preference denge kyunki unhe GST input tax credit (ITC) milta hai.
  - **Fayda 2:** Razorpay live account approval instant milta hai.
  - *Note: Consult your local Ahmedabad CA (Chartered Accountant) for quarterly filing.*

## 4. Standard SaaS Agreement: Key Clauses to Protect You

Jab aap kisi restaurant se deal karein, toh ek simple 2-page agreement sign karwayein:

- **Data Ownership Clause:** Restaurant ka customer data aur order history unka proprietary data hai. QLLEX sirf data processor hai.
- **Limitation of Liability Clause (Must Have!):**
  - *"In no event shall QLLEX Technologies be liable for any lost food profits, kitchen delays, customer disputes, or server downtime exceeding the total subscription fee paid by the customer in the preceding 1 month."*
  - Yeh clause aapko kisi bhi anchahe court dispute se 100% legal immunity provide karta hai!
- **Payment Terms:** Subscriptions are prepaid. Service will be paused after a 5-day grace period for unpaid accounts.

## 5. Intellectual Property (IP) Protection

- **Source Code Privacy:** Apne GitHub repositories ko hamesha **Private** rakhein.
- **Copyright Notice:** Har frontend aur backend file ke top par comment add karein:
  ```javascript
  /*
   * (C) Copyright 2026 QLLEX Technologies. All Rights Reserved.
   * Proprietary and Confidential. Unauthorized copying or redistribution is strictly prohibited.
   */
  ```
- **Trademark:** Jab aap 20 customers cross kar lein, toh "QLLEX" brand name ko Class 42 (Computer Software Services) ke under trademark application file kar dein (~₹4,500 govt fee).
